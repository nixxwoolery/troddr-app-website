# TRODDR: Jamaica Travel Guide and Trip Planner

> Jamaica's curated travel guide with locally vetted restaurants, hidden beaches, boutique stays, and trip planning in one free app.

Discover places to eat, stay and explore across Jamaica. TRODDR's local recommendations cover Kingston, Montego Bay, Negril, Ocho Rios, Portland and Treasure Beach. Browse travel guides, save places and build trips in the app.

Things to Do in Jamaica

Build your Jamaica trip around a few areas you can enjoy properly. Use these destination ideas and TRODDR guides to combine an activity, a meal and time to explore without packing every day with long transfers.

Explore Kingston’s culture and food

Use Kingston as the focus of a city day: choose a cultural stop, leave time for lunch and finish with a relaxed evening. Our things to do in Kingston guide covers museums, art, gardens and a suggested one-day route. Pair it with the brunch collection or date-night food trail.

Take a mountain break above Kingston

For a change of pace, explore Irish Town and Holywell. TRODDR’s A Day in the Clouds guide brings together this mountain outing. Make it a separate day from your downtown plans so there is room for lunch and a slower journey. Check conditions and access before setting out.

Plan a Negril or Ocho Rios weekend

If your base is Negril, use the 48 hours in Negril guide to shape a weekend around beach time and food. If you are staying around Ocho Rios, start with 48 hours in Ocho Rios. Choose one main outing each day, then keep nearby food stops flexible.

A weekend guide is a framework, not a requirement to tick off every stop. Decide what matters most to your group before adding another excursion.

Make room for Portland

Frenchman’s Cove in San San is one of the places in TRODDR’s Portland collection. Use it as a starting point for exploring this part of the island, with a nearby food stop rather than a second distant destination. Check admission and access directly before travelling, and keep an alternative plan for your day.

Follow a South Coast food trail

Make food part of the outing with the South Coast food stops guide. Treasure Beach’s JackSprat Restaurant is included in TRODDR’s destination collection. If a quieter break is your priority, browse Refresh and Renew for another way to plan your time.

How to choose what fits your trip

Begin with your accommodation and available days. Group nearby places together, allow for transfers and leave unscheduled time. If you have only a weekend, concentrate on one area. For a longer visit, change bases only when it gives you time to explore a different region.

Confirm activity hours, bookings, weather conditions and transport before finalising plans. Look at our where to eat in Jamaica guide to add meal stops, then save your choices in TRODDR.

Common questions

How do I choose things to do in Jamaica?

Choose an area close to your accommodation, then combine one main activity with nearby food and time to relax. Use the destination guides on this page to narrow your choices.

What can I do in Jamaica over a weekend?

Focus on one area. TRODDR has 48-hour guides for Negril and Ocho Rios, plus Kingston food guides and an Irish Town and Holywell mountain guide.

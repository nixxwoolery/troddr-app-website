Where to eat in Kingston

Start with Devon House Bakery for a casual stop, or build a shortlist around Broken Plate, Chilitos Jamexican and Cannonball Café. Danya’s Coffee Barrel is another option when your plans take you downtown. Pick a neighbourhood first so that lunch fits your route rather than becoming a separate cross-city trip.

For a morning outing, browse our Kingston brunch guide. For an evening with several stops, use the Kingston date-night restaurant guide. Combine your meal with our Kingston activity ideas.

Montego Bay and Ocho Rios restaurants

In Montego Bay, TRODDR’s collection includes Sugar Mill Restaurant, Delmare, Marguerites and Mystic Thai. In Ocho Rios, start with Christophers at Hermosa Cove, Evita’s Italian Restaurant or Ciao Bella Art Cafe & Restaurant. Treat these as starting points to compare menus, location and the kind of evening you want.

If you are planning more than a meal, the 48 hours in Ocho Rios guide helps bring your stops together.

Food stops in Negril

Best in the West, Coletta’s Restaurant and Chill Pops Gourmet Paletas are among the places in TRODDR’s Negril collection. Decide whether your meal belongs near your beach day or on a separate outing. Our 48 hours in Negril guide is a starting point for a weekend plan.

Portland and the South Coast

Look up King Corn Crab Shack in Long Bay for a Portland stop, or JackSprat Restaurant in Treasure Beach when you are on the South Coast. Avoid choosing a restaurant on the other side of the island just for one meal; save it for a day when you are already travelling that route.

Explore the South Coast food stops guide for a food-led road trip, or the sushi in Jamaica guide if you have a particular craving.

Before you choose a restaurant

Check the current menu, opening hours, reservation policy and dietary options directly with the venue. Ask whether prices include service charges and tax, and confirm payment options. For groups, agree on the budget before you book. For a food trail, leave room between stops and choose a mix of small bites and one main meal.

This is a selection from TRODDR’s existing collections, rather than a live availability list. Open the app to save places and arrange your shortlist on a map.

Common questions

Where should I start when looking for food in Jamaica?

Start with the town or area where you will spend the day, then compare menus and travel distance. Kingston, Montego Bay, Ocho Rios, Negril, Portland and Treasure Beach all have restaurant ideas on this page.

Can I plan a restaurant outing in Kingston?

Yes. TRODDR has separate brunch and date-night guides for Kingston. Choose a route with nearby stops and confirm reservations directly with each venue.
